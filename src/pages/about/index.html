Who is TPA?

A 501(c)(3), Detroit-based grassroots organization driven by residents — improving the lives of poor Detroiters through advocacy and direct programming.

Our mission

Human rights.
Human dignity.

“Our mission is to vastly improve the socioeconomic condition of Blacks and other historically oppressed people. We take action and fight for the human rights and dignity of all human beings facing exploitation and injustice.”

We work through advocacy and direct programming in five key areas — Community Development, Direct Resources, Basic Needs, Community Advocacy and Safety — all under our program, Adopt The Block.

Adopt The Block’s mission is to build a safe, thriving community by partnering with residents through participatory practices that increase their engagement in their own neighborhoods while they become active in solving community problems.

On the record

Milestones

Moments TPA and its divisions have reported on this site over the years.

  1. 2016 · Proposal A

    The organization’s political action committee campaigned for the Community Benefits Agreement, Proposal A — winning Detroit districts 2 and 3.

  2. 2017 · Tax exemption workshop

    Detroit Peoples Community assisted over thirty families at its Poverty Property Tax Exemption Workshop, and ran “Adopt The Block” as a summer clean-up initiative.

  3. 2018 · Entrepreneur workshop

    The Peoples Economy launched its first large-scale Entrepreneur Workshop on April 21, 2018, helping incorporate more than ten start-up businesses.

Leadership

Meet the team

Organizers who come from the communities they serve.

Negus Vu

President

Read bio

Negus Vu, founder and president of Detroit Peoples Community, was once a high-school dropout. In his early twenties he suffered from the lack of job opportunities and exposure that led him down a narrow path. He now combats the same cultural deviance that plagued his development for years through community organizing centered on direct services and personal transformational leadership. Vu has a background in Biology from Wayne State University and owns and operates several small businesses.

Jacqueline Robinson

Chief Operation Officer

Read bio

Jacqueline Robinson is the vice president of Detroit Peoples Community and holds a bachelor’s degree in Social Work. She was previously employed with the State of Michigan and has a background in case management, specifically with children and families. She also owns a home health agency and has used her experience to help fellow entrepreneurs develop their own business plans. Jackie’s passion is helping underserved Detroiters by directing them to resources that can resolve their problems.

Sam Miller

Community Activist

Read bio

Sam Miller, activist and rap artist, grew up on the east side of Detroit in what is known as the “Redzone.” At an early age Sam experienced the hardship of growing up around gang culture and violence, and he works to inspire directly impacted people to be self-determined and positive. He holds an Associate’s degree in General Studies and a Bachelor’s in Aviation Management Technology from Eastern Michigan University, and became a pilot while attending Davis Aerospace Technical High School. Once a member of New Era Detroit in 2015, Sam now serves as a galvanizer for The Peoples Action.
