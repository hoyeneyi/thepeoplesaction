The mission

A safe, thriving community — built with residents

Adopt The Block’s mission is to build a safe, thriving community by partnering with residents through participatory practices — increasing their level of engagement in their own neighborhoods while they become active in solving community problems.

We welcome you to join us in changing the narrative of Detroit for the betterment of our neighborhoods.

Detroit Peoples Community ran Adopt The Block as a summer clean-up initiative aimed at building value in neighborhoods. Today all five of TPA’s focus areas fall under it.

Five focus areas

What “adopting” a block means

Each area connects to work TPA and its divisions already do on the ground.

  1. Community Development

    Revitalizing the neighborhood through landscaping and clean-ups, and helping residents organize block clubs.

  2. Direct Resources

    Tangible resources for residents, with resources and program updates available at our community meetings.

  3. Basic Needs

    Provisions that benefit the underserved, such as food and other resources.

  4. Community Advocacy

    Open dialogue at community meetings to create practical solutions, plus voter education through The Peoples Politics.

  5. Safety

    Self-defense, violence-reduction tactics and search parties for missing citizens through The Peoples Army.
