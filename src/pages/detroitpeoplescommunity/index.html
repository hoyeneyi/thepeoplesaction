From our history

Thirty families, one workshop

In 2017, Detroit Peoples Community assisted over thirty families at our Poverty Property Tax Exemption Workshop. That same year our summer program, “Adopt The Block,” ran as a clean-up initiative aimed at building value in neighborhoods.
