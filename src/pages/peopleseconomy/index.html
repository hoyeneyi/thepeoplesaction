Homeowners

Keep your home. Know your exemption.

The Peoples Action provides workshops to help save homeowners from losing their homes to tax foreclosure. We host workshops for the Homeowners Property Tax Assistance Program (HPTAP) application.

If you are a homeowner with a low annual income, you may qualify to have your property taxes exempted or reduced for the calendar year. Our Action Members assist Detroit homeowners with the application process — explaining what to bring and helping get the necessary documents copied and printed.
