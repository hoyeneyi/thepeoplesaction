Who is TPA?

Resident-driven.
Detroit-rooted.

The Peoples Action is a 501(c)(3), Detroit-based grassroots organization dedicated to improving the lives of poor Detroiters through advocacy and direct programming in five key areas — all under our program, Adopt The Block.

“Our mission is to vastly improve the socioeconomic condition of Blacks and other historically oppressed people. We take action and fight for the human rights and dignity of all human beings facing exploitation and injustice.”

Join us in our endeavor to make history in Detroit and across the nation — making it a brighter future for everyday people.

Our core program

Adopt The Block:
five focus areas

Adopt The Block partners with residents through participatory practices that increase their engagement in their own neighborhoods — and their role in solving community problems.

  1. Community Development

    Revitalizing neighborhoods through landscaping, clean-ups and organized block clubs.

  2. Direct Resources

    Getting tangible resources into residents’ hands — and connecting them to the help they need.

  3. Basic Needs

    Provisions that benefit the underserved, such as food and other essentials.

  4. Community Advocacy

    Community meetings, open dialogue and voter education that turn concerns into practical solutions.

  5. Safety

    Violence-prevention, self-defense training and search parties for missing community members.
